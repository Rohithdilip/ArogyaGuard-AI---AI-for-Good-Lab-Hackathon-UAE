TRANSPARENT PROVENANCE

Knowledge Base.

Essential Indian healthcare terms

ASHA
Accredited Social Health Activist. A community health worker who connects families with care.
NHM
National Health Mission. India's public health programme framework.
ANC
Antenatal Care. Healthcare visits and support during pregnancy.
JSSK
Janani Shishu Suraksha Karyakram. Covers specified free maternal services and transport.
PMSMA
Pradhan Mantri Surakshit Matritva Abhiyan. Supports antenatal care and identifying high-risk pregnancies.
SUMAN
Surakshit Matritva Aashwasan. Promotes respectful, assured maternity and newborn care.

How the agents work together.
